📢 Publication Alert!  
I am happy to share that our paper, "Mid-Season Parcel-Based Crop Type Mapping Using Multi-Source Time Series of Big Earth Observations and Deep Learning," has been published in the Big Earth Data journal. 🌱🛰  
Accurate, timely crop-type maps matter for crop inventories, agricultural monitoring, and sustainable resource management. But what matters most is having crop-type maps during the growing season (not after it ends) when they can actually inform decisions.  
  
Here is what we did and what we found:  
🔹 We trained six models, including four deep learning (Transformers, hybrid LSTM–Transformers, 1D CNN, and LSTM) and two machine learning baselines (Random Forest and XGBoost), on multi-temporal Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 data from 2020–2024 to map major crops (corn, soybean, wheat, hay, and others) across Quebec.  
🔹 We used field boundaries and crop labels from La Financière Agricole du Québec (FADQ) as reference data.  
🔹 The Transformer performed best on the Sentinel-1/2 data, reaching 0.97 overall accuracy on validation and 0.93 on the test set.  
  
And two findings I found especially exciting:  
✅ Transferability to an unseen year. Applied to entirely unseen 2025 data, the trained models produced mid-season crop maps with ~92% overall accuracy with no additional in-year training data. This directly tackles one of the field's biggest open challenges: uncertainty around future inference.  
The takeaway: reliable, mid-season, parcel-based crop mapping is achievable without collecting new field data every year, which is a step toward more practical, operational agricultural monitoring.  
  
✅ Foundation models show real promise. Using Google's AlphaEarth embeddings with classical ML (RF/XGB), we achieved accuracy comparable to the deep learning models on Sentinel data.  
Worth noting: AlphaEarth is an annual product, so we included it to gauge how well these embeddings work for crop mapping rather than as a direct mid-season equivalent.  
  
My sincere thanks to my co-authors for their guidance throughout this work. 🙏
