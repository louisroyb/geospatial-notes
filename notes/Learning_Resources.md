# Learning Resources

**Summary**: Courses, tutorials, lectures, and training material for Earth observation and geospatial work.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

---

- [Unsupervised Classification with Satellite Embeddings](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/tutorials/community/satellite-embedding-02-unsupervised-classification): The second tutorial in the Spatial Thoughts satellite embedding series, mapping crops without any field labels. The workflow runs from picking a region (Cerro Gordo County, Iowa), loading and filtering the Satellite Embedding dataset, masking to cropland with the USDA NASS Cropland Data Layer, drawing stratified random samples, clustering with `wekaCascadeKMeans()`, then labelling clusters by comparing their areas against published crop statistics and validating against the CDL. The trick is that it substitutes "local knowledge of the region along with aggregate crop statistics", which exist almost everywhere, for field samples, which do not. *Keywords: unsupervised classification, satellite embeddings, wekaCascadeKMeans, Cropland Data Layer, Iowa, label-free crop mapping*
  - Related: [[Embeddings]], [[Agriculture]], [[Google_Earth_Engine]]

- [Free Earth Data Science Courses & Textbooks](https://earthdatascience.org/courses/): Free Earth Data Science Courses & Textbooks. From Earth Lab at the University of Colorado Boulder, publishing "free and open earth data science textbooks and courses as open education resources", much of it developed for their Certificate and Master's programmes in Earth Data Analytics. Covers remote sensing (70 lessons), spatial data and GIS (106), reproducible science and programming (214), data exploration (41) and time series (22), delivered as textbooks (Introduction to and Intermediate Earth Data Science, a Python plotting guide), full courses (Earth Analytics Bootcamp, Python, R) and modular self-paced lessons. Python-first with some R, plus Git, GitHub, Bash and Jupyter. All free under CC BY-SA 4.0. *Keywords: Earth Lab, CU Boulder, free textbooks, reproducible science, python, open education*
  - Related: [[Python]], [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Data]]

- [Spectral Indices for Land and Aquatic Applications](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/learn/trainings/spectral-indices-land-aquatic-applications): Spectral Indices course. A NASA ARSET training, three one-hour instructor-led online sessions run twice daily, 26 October to 9 November 2023. Part 1 introduces NDVI and its calculation, Part 2 covers aquatic indices (NDCI, NDAVI, FAI, NDTI) and Part 3 land indices (EVI, SAVI, NBR), worked with Landsat 9, Sentinel-2 and HLS data through Google Earth Engine exercises and NASA DEVELOP case studies. Assumes the Fundamentals of Remote Sensing training first. *Keywords: NASA ARSET, spectral indices, NDVI EVI SAVI NBR, aquatic indices, HLS, training*
  - Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Google_Earth_Engine]]

- [Machine Learning for Beginners - A Curriculum](https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners): Machine Learning for Beginners - A Curriculum. Recomended by Raymond Besiga. Microsoft Cloud Advocates' "12 weeks, 26 lessons, 52 quizzes, classic Machine Learning for all", deliberately excluding deep learning, which sits in their separate AI for Beginners course. Covers regression, classification, clustering, NLP, time series forecasting and reinforcement learning using real-world datasets drawn from a range of global cultures. Python with scikit-learn primarily, R Markdown alternatives in the solution folders, plus Jupyter notebooks, web app projects and a quiz app. MIT, 50+ translations, and at ~90k stars by far the most starred repository in these notes. *Keywords: machine learning curriculum, Microsoft, beginner, scikit-learn, 26 lessons, free course*
  - Related: [[Machine_Learning]], [[Code_Repositories]], [[Python]]

- [Satellite Embedding Deep Dive (Full Workshop)](https://courses.spatialthoughts.com/gee-satellite-embedding.html#crop-type-mapping): Satellite Embedding Deep Dive (Full Workshop). Ujaval Gandhi's hands-on workshop on Google's Satellite Embedding V1 dataset, the embeddings produced by DeepMind's AlphaEarth Foundations model, worked entirely in Earth Engine. Four main modules: visualizing embeddings and unsupervised clustering, crop type mapping, mapping mangroves by supervised classification, and object detection through similarity search (finding brick kilns). The crop type section detects corn and soybean in Iowa by clustering embeddings against a cropland mask with stratified sampling, then validates against official crop statistics — crop typing without a large labeled training set. Supplementary modules cover water detection, urban tree classification and polygon clustering. *Keywords: AlphaEarth, satellite embedding, unsupervised clustering, crop type mapping, similarity search, Earth Engine workshop*
  - Video recording: [Satellite Embedding Deep Dive (Full Workshop, Ad-Free)](https://youtu.be/vDWjfW7pylM) - the same workshop on YouTube.
  - Related: [[Embeddings]], [[Google_Earth_Engine]], [[Agriculture]]

- [Introduction to R and Geographic Information Systems (GIS)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/introduction-to-r-and-gis-fall-2023/): Introduction to R and Geographic Information Systems (GIS). MIT OpenCourseWare RES.1-002, taught by Dr. Helena Vallicrosa in Civil and Environmental Engineering, Fall 2023, non-credit. Covers R basics, navigating the interface, handling different data formats, running and interpreting linear models, and doing GIS work in R. Material was originally built for an ecosystem ecology course. Includes lecture videos, programming assignments and supporting files. *Keywords: R, GIS, MIT OpenCourseWare, linear models, ecosystem ecology, free course*
  - Related: [[Data]]

- [CS 598 GeoSpatial AI — GeoAI Foundation Models and Embeddings](https://mediaspace.illinois.edu/media/t/1_6ttvnnnu): Lecture on foundation models. A recorded guest lecture by Adam Stewart, given 2 April 2026 in Arindam Banerjee's CS 598 GeoSpatial Artificial Intelligence course at UIUC (Spring 2026). The course spans classical geostatistics through modern deep learning for spatial and spatiotemporal data — Gaussian processes and kriging, self-supervised learning for EO, graph neural networks, transfer learning, and generative models. Worth noting the author link: Stewart led SSL4EO-L, whose sampling code is noted on [[Foundation_Models]]. *Keywords: GeoAI, foundation models, embeddings, guest lecture, UIUC CS598, Adam Stewart*
  - Related: [[Foundation_Models]], [[Embeddings]]

- [Python Foundation for Spatial Analysis, December 2025 (Online)](https://spatialthoughts.com/training/python_foundation_20251202/): Protected: Python Foundation for Spatial Analysis, December 2025 (Online). The session page for a December 2025 online cohort of Ujaval Gandhi's Spatial Thoughts course; the page is password protected for enrolled participants, so only the public course description applies here. The course teaches Python from the basics for GIS practitioners with no programming background, covering programming concepts, spatial libraries, geospatial APIs and building spatial data processing pipelines. Full material — notebooks, datasets and ad-free videos — is free for self-study, with paid live instructor-led cohorts and certification through the academy. *Keywords: Python, spatial analysis, Spatial Thoughts, beginner course, geospatial APIs, protected page*
  - Related: [[Python]], [[Data]]

- [Leanpub course bookstore](https://leanpub.com/bookstore?type=course): Leanpub is a digital publishing platform built on "Publish Early. Publish Often." — authors write in Markdown or upload PDF/EPUB, can sync from GitHub or Dropbox, and publish while still writing, on 80% royalties. Beyond ebooks it carries bundles, courses, tracks and a podcast, so the course filter here surfaces interactive learning material rather than books. *Keywords: Leanpub, self-publishing, online courses, markdown, learning platform, ebooks*
  - Related: [[Learning_Resources]]

- [Introduction to the Satellite Embedding Dataset](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/tutorials/community/satellite-embedding-01-introduction): Spatial Thoughts. Earth Engine community tutorial introducing Google's AlphaEarth Foundations embedding dataset — 64-band annual images where each pixel vector encodes that year's multi-sensor time series, available from 2017 onward. First in a series covering classification, regression, change detection, and similarity search. *Keywords: AlphaEarth, satellite embeddings, Google Earth Engine, tutorial, similarity search, 64 bands*
  - Related: [[Embeddings]], [[Google_Earth_Engine]], [[Remote_Sensing]]

- [ESA Stakeholder Engagement Facility — Training](https://esa-sef.eu/training/): Satkeholder engagement facility trainng courses. The SEF exists to raise uptake and long-term impact of ESA-funded Earth observation solutions by keeping engagement going with new and existing stakeholders. It runs remote and in-person training on using EO data and applications, either standalone or bolted onto an existing event, and keeps an archive of past courses and case studies. Key thematic areas include Food Systems and Ecosystems & Biodiversity, with project environments set up through APEx. The site now resolves at sef.apex.esa.int. *Keywords: ESA, capacity building, EO training, stakeholder engagement, APEx, food systems*
  - Companion link from the note: [AGENDA & RESOURCES](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DZ-_0Gv5sgtyA-IGCIirwLB7lRgjr2-YH2h7lAVzbTs/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.v18pd7uskerw) — a Google Doc agenda and resource list; contents were not readable without access.
  - Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Agriculture]]

- [EO College Courses](https://eo-college.org/courses/): EO College. A free online learning platform for Earth observation, tagline "Digital learning, sustainable future". The catalogue spans radar remote sensing (Basic Principles of Radar Backscatter, Winter-Water-Warming SAR applications), hyperspectral imaging (the "Beyond the Visible" series on forest, soil and agricultural uses), the "Land in Focus" collection on urban spaces, ecosystems, agriculture and hazards, "Towards Zero Hunger" on food security, and an Introduction to Machine Learning for Earth Observation. Some content comes from partners such as HYPERedu. *Keywords: EO College, free courses, SAR, hyperspectral, machine learning, earth observation training*
  - Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Machine_Learning]]

- [Copernicus MOOC](https://mooc.copernicus.eu/): Copernius Massive Open Online Course (MOOC). EU-funded free online training on using Copernicus data and services to address societal challenges and build business opportunities, aimed at people who could benefit from geo-information data but have little or no prior experience. The platform hosts several courses, including a six-week "AI for Earth Monitoring" MOOC built jointly by EUMETSAT, ECMWF, Mercator Ocean International and the EEA, and one on monitoring atmospheric composition. *Keywords: Copernicus, MOOC, free training, AI for earth monitoring, EUMETSAT ECMWF, geo-information*
  - Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Machine_Learning]]

- [E-TRAINEE Course](https://3dgeo-heidelberg.github.io/etrainee/index.html): Heidelberg University. An open e-learning course on "Time Series Analysis in Remote Sensing for Understanding Human-Environment Interactions", built by Charles University, Heidelberg University, University of Innsbruck and University of Warsaw under an ERASMUS+ partnership. Four modules cover satellite multispectral time series, 3D/4D point clouds, airborne hyperspectral data, and laboratory spectroscopy, aimed at MSc students in geoinformatics and geography. *Keywords: time series analysis, e-learning, hyperspectral, point clouds, open courseware, change analysis*
  - Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Data]]
