# Community Resources

**Summary**: People, labs, newsletters, and curated lists worth following in the geospatial and GeoAI community.
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

---

- [GeoSpatial ML](https://geospatialml.com/): GeoSpatial ML Weekly dispatches on geospatial machine learning — remote sensing, earth observation, and everything in between Isaac Corley & Caleb Robinson. A weekly newsletter run by Isaac Corley and Caleb Robinson covering geospatial machine learning, remote sensing and Earth observation. *Keywords: newsletter, geospatial machine learning, weekly, Isaac Corley, Caleb Robinson, earth observation*
  - Related: [[Machine_Learning]], [[Remote_Sensing]]

- [Isaac Corley](https://isaac.earth/): Isaac Corley Director of AI/ML Research Taylor Geospatial. Personal site of a GeoAI researcher working on "computer vision and foundation models for earth observation—from research through production", with a PhD in Electrical Engineering from UTSA and past roles at Microsoft Research, BlackSky and Wherobots. Lists open-source work including TorchGeo, Segmentation Models PyTorch and Fields of the World, 15+ recent papers at CVPR, ICLR and NeurIPS, plus talks and podcast appearances. *Keywords: Isaac Corley, Taylor Geospatial, GeoAI, foundation models, TorchGeo, Fields of the World*
  - Related: [[Foundation_Models]], [[Agriculture]], [[Deep_Learning]]

- [Precision AG Lab](https://paglab.org/tour/): Precision AG Lab - Prof. Dr. Kang Yu. A research lab on precision agriculture led by Prof. Dr. Kang Yu, positioning itself as "your testing ground for future farming" under the motto "Discover the truth, develop the technology, deploy the practice, by exploring your new ideas together!". The site carries news, team, events, publications, data and project sections. *Keywords: precision agriculture, research lab, Kang Yu, farming technology, publications, data*
  - Related: [[Agriculture]]

- [Awesome-Geospatial](https://github.com/sacridini/Awesome-Geospatial): Long list of geospatial analysis tools. Geospatial analysis, or just spatial analysis. A curated list running to 50+ categories and hundreds of entries: databases (PostGIS), libraries (GDAL, GEOS, PROJ), classification and atmospheric correction software, GIS applications, web mapping frameworks, tile servers, LiDAR and SAR tooling, deep learning, per-language sections for Python, R, Java, JavaScript, Go, C++ and 20 more, cloud services, plus data sources, conferences, podcasts and cheat sheets. CC0-1.0, and one of the most starred things in these notes at 5,270. *Keywords: awesome list, geospatial tools, curated directory, GDAL PostGIS, per-language, reference*
  - Related: [[Code_Repositories]], [[Data]]
