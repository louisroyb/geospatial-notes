# Careers and Research

**Summary**: Job portals, vacancies and professional training, plus free material on research skills — thesis writing, methods, statistics and reference management.
**Last updated**: 2026-10-02

---

- [Free online dissertation and thesis repositories](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/yusuf-yakubu-yusuf-057675122_do-not-use-chatgpt-to-write-your-research-share-7482754172984070144-SGCF): LinkedIn post by Yusuf Yakubu Yusuf, 14 July 2026: *"Do not use ChatGPT to write your research project. Instead, visit these sites, download completed theses, & learn how it is done."* It lists 20 open-access thesis repositories; EBSCO is listed twice, so 19 are unique. They include [OATD](https://www.oatd.org/), [EBSCO Open Dissertations](https://opendissertations.org/), [NDLTD](https://ndltd.org/), [DiVA](https://www.diva-portal.org/), the British Library's [EThOS](https://www.bl.uk/collection/ethos), [MIT theses](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/7582), [OhioLINK ETD](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/), [CaltechTHESIS](https://thesis.library.caltech.edu/), [Harvard DASH](https://dash.harvard.edu/), [TSpace (Toronto)](https://tspace.library.utoronto.ca/), [Helsinki E-thesis](https://ethesis.helsinki.fi/en) and [Pretoria](https://repository.up.ac.za/handle/2263/31741). *Keywords: theses, dissertations, open access, OATD, research writing, repositories*
  - Source: [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/yusuf-yakubu-yusuf-057675122_do-not-use-chatgpt-to-write-your-research-share-7482754172984070144-SGCF); see [[LinkedIn]].

- [18 free research courses for PhD students](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dr-bikash-chandra-taly-4089a0152_the-research-clinic-share-7481669766865240064-LMEX): LinkedIn post by Dr. Bikash Chandra Taly, 11 July 2026: *"No subscriptions or paid certificates are required to access the learning materials."* Free courses in six groups:
  - **Research methods**: OpenLearn's [research perspectives](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/understanding-different-research-perspectives/content-section-0), an [MIT doctoral seminar](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-347-doctoral-seminar-in-research-methods-i-fall-2004/), Yale qualitative methods.
  - **Academic writing**: [MIT graduate technical writing](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21w-794-graduate-technical-writing-workshop-january-iap-2019/), [Purdue OWL](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/index.html).
  - **Statistics**: [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability), [MIT 15.075](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-075j-statistical-thinking-and-data-analysis-fall-2011/), [StatQuest](https://statquest.org/video-index/).
  - **Systematic reviews**: [PAHO](https://campus.paho.org/en/course/introduction-systematic-reviews), [CMU OLI](https://oli.cmu.edu/courses/systematic-reviews-and-meta-analysis-o-f/).
  - **Reference management**: [Cornell](https://guides.library.cornell.edu/zoterotutorial) and [official](https://www.zotero.org/support/screencast_tutorials) Zotero tutorials.
  - **Research ethics**: [OpenLearn](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/education-development/becoming-ethical-researcher), the US ORI's [The Lab](https://ori.hhs.gov/the-lab) and [Research Clinic](https://ori.hhs.gov/research-clinic).
  - *Keywords: PhD skills, research methods, academic writing, statistics, Zotero, research ethics*
  - Source: [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dr-bikash-chandra-taly-4089a0152_the-research-clinic-share-7481669766865240064-LMEX); see [[LinkedIn]].

- [World Bank Group careers](https://worldbankgroup.csod.com/ux/ats/careersite/1/home?c=worldbankgroup): The World Bank Group's official job portal, a Cornerstone OnDemand applicant tracking system where all WBG vacancies are posted. That includes staff roles, the Young Professionals Program and the Pioneer internships (e.g. Environment Intern). Background is on [worldbank.org/careers](https://www.worldbank.org/ext/en/careers). The `c=worldbankgroup` parameter is part of the address, not tracking. *Keywords: World Bank, jobs, Young Professionals Program, internships, international development, careers portal*
  - The portal is a JavaScript app; this description comes from World Bank pages and search.

- [2026 MENA Regional Access Training — UN OCHA](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rawan-taha_apply-share-girlfromlinkedin-ugcPost-7477790910769074178-6EEc): Reshared on LinkedIn by Rawan Taha, 30 June 2026, from a post by Adrianna Korte-Nahabedian: *"A training with United Nations OCHA for mid career humanitarians."* A 5-day residential course in Jordan, 26 September – 1 October 2026, supported by ZIF, on humanitarian access principles, access coordination and negotiation. It was open to mid-level staff from OCHA, UN agencies and NGOs in the region. **Applications closed on 6 July 2026** and the course has run. Not geospatial. *Keywords: UN OCHA, humanitarian access, negotiation, MENA, professional training, Jordan*
  - Source: [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rawan-taha_apply-share-girlfromlinkedin-ugcPost-7477790910769074178-6EEc); see [[LinkedIn]].
